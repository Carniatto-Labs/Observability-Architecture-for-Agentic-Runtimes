# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas on their technical merits.
- Give actionable feedback and welcome differing viewpoints.
- Protect confidential information, personal data, and security-sensitive material.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private, confidential, or security-sensitive information.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
